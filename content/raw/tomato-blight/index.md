Brown spots with yellow rings showed up on the lower leaves of all three tomato plants. That pattern is early blight, a fungus that splashes up from the soil.

I removed the affected leaves, added mulch under the plants, and switched to watering at the base only. More about the beds in [the garden post](/blog/starting-a-vegetable-garden/).
