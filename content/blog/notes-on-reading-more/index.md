For years I read maybe two books a year. This year I'm on track for twelve, and nothing dramatic changed.

## Always have the next book ready

Finishing a book and not knowing what to read next used to mean weeks without reading. Now there is always a short list on the [books page](/books/).

## Read in small slots

Ten pages before bed adds up to a book every few weeks. An e-reader such as the [Kindle Paperwhite](https://example.com/kindle) makes those small slots easier, since it fits in a jacket pocket.

## Get a library card

Borrowing removes the pressure to finish a book just because I paid for it. More on that in [a short note](/raw/library-card/).
