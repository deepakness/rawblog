We rented a small cabin two hours from the city for a long weekend. There was no Wi-Fi and barely any phone signal, which turned out to be the best part.

## Day one

The first trail was a 9 km loop through pine forest to a lake. It took about three and a half hours with a long lunch break at the water.

## Day two

The second day was a steeper climb to a ridge with a view across the valley. Fog rolled in around noon, so we turned back early.

## What I packed

1. Layers: a base layer, a fleece, and a rain shell
2. Food: sandwiches, fruit, and the [usual trail snacks](/raw/trail-snacks/)
3. Navigation: an offline map on the phone and a paper map as backup
4. A headlamp, which we did end up needing on the way back

> The best trips are the ones where you forget to check the time.
