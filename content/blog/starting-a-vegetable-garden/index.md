This spring I built two raised beds in the backyard. Each one is 1.2 m by 2.4 m and about 30 cm deep, filled with a mix of topsoil and compost.

![Two wooden raised beds with young plants](./raised-beds.jpg)

## What I planted

I kept the first season simple and picked vegetables that are hard to get wrong.

- Tomatoes, three plants, staked from day one
- Lettuce and spinach, sown in short rows every two weeks
- Bush beans along the sunny edge
- Basil and parsley in the corners

## Watering

A soaker hose on a cheap timer runs for 20 minutes every morning. Watering early keeps the leaves dry during the day, which helps with mildew.

## What I'd do differently

The beds get about six hours of direct sun, which is enough for leafy greens but on the low side for tomatoes. Next year the tomatoes go in a container on the south side of the house. I wrote a short note about [the blight that hit them in August](/raw/tomato-blight/).

**Update**: Nov 02, 2026

The beds are now covered with a layer of shredded leaves for the winter. The lettuce is still going under a row cover.
